# Flow: Narrative & Character

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Use when | Third-person story, case study, customer/team journey, or event-driven narrative with external stakes |
| Do not use | First-person memoir (`personal-reflective.md`) or pure opinion/framework (`thesis-perspective.md`) |
| Ethics rule | Distinguish honesty from exposure; protect the dignity and privacy of real people appearing in the story |

| Stage | Focus | Questions / Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Character Stakes | Cost of stasis | What does it cost the character to remain who they are? Why must this story be told now? |
| 2. Friction | Earned change | What does the protagonist **give up**? What did they almost do instead? Could the right action still fail? |
| 3. Reward Timing | Avoid parable trap | Does the universe validate the character too quickly? Can the story hold ambiguity after the choice? |
| 4. Scene Proof | Concrete evidence | Which specific scene *proves* the truth rather than summarizing it? What lingers after the climax? |
| 5. Responsibility | Collateral check | Is this our story to tell? Who else pays the price for telling it? Anonymize or protect third parties |
| 6. Research | Recognition | Search real expressions (`search_web`) so readers recognize themselves rather than admiring from afar |

| Assessment Field | What to Produce Before Gate 1 |
|---|---|
| Truth & character cost | The uncomfortable truth and what staying unchanged costs the protagonist |
| Reader recognition | Specific reader situation + practitioner vocabulary from research |
| Friction & reward check | What is sacrificed + confirmation that transformation is earned, not declared |
| Scene proof & ethics | The proving scene + responsibility flag for any third parties involved |
| Gate 1 check | Wait for user confirmation of the root conflict and responsibility boundaries |

| Post-Gate Step | Action | Output / Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Format selection | Confirm guide in `story-formats/` (`longform/personal-essay.md`, `linkedin/`, etc.) | Format confirmed |
| Story Brief | Present opening conflict, turning scene, cost paid, and closing image | **Gate 2:** Wait for user approval before drafting |

```text
Stakes & Cost -> Friction & Ethics -> Research -> Gate 1 (Conflict) -> Format -> Story Brief -> Gate 2 (Brief) -> Draft
```
