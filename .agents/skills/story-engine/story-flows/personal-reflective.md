# Flow: Personal & Reflective

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Use when | Lived personal experience, memoir, setback, turning point, or founder confession |
| Do not use | Analytical/contrarian POV with no personal scene (`thesis-perspective.md`) or symbolic world (`world-allegory.md`) |
| Evidence rule | Separate remembered facts, feelings at the time, and later interpretations; never invent dialogue or sensory details |

| Stage | Focus | Questions / Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Recover | Facts vs. feelings | What happened and in what order? What could an observer have seen? Which dialogue is exact vs. approximate? |
| 2. Desire & Belief | Hidden expectation | What did you want then? Why did losing or getting it matter? What did you believe the outcome said about you? |
| 3. Contradiction | Dual pull | What two desires or obligations pulled you in opposite directions (e.g., affection vs. resentment, relief vs. grief)? |
| 4. Integrity | Honesty & loss | What truth are you afraid to say? Which detail feels most dangerous? What did the narrator **give up** (not just discover)? |
| 5. Scene Proof | Earned truth | Which single concrete moment *proves* the truth? Does the final line earn silence instead of explaining the lesson? |
| 6. Research | Recognition | Search real expressions (`search_web`) for unpolished reader vocabulary; avoid writing to impress |

| Craft Lens | Technique | When to Apply |
|---|---|---|
| Event / Meaning | Separate external situation from internal question | When plot details obscure what actually changed inside |
| Broken Expectation | Examine the belief beneath disappointment | When setback feels small externally but shook identity |
| Factual Pressure | Let concrete objects/actions carry emotion | When prose leans on adjectives or tells the reader what to feel |
| Judgment / Action Gap | Link public role, fear of humiliation, and choice | When narrator acted against their own judgment |
| Experience / Guidance | Ground practical advice in examined struggle | When reflection bridges into actionable guidance |

| Assessment Field | What to Produce Before Gate 1 |
|---|---|
| Core wound & truth resisted | One-sentence uncomfortable truth the narrator avoids |
| Reader recognition | Specific person and situation (not demographics) + real vocabulary from research |
| What narrator loses | Concrete illusion, status, or comfort surrendered |
| Scene proof & dangerous detail | The proving moment and the raw detail worth keeping |
| Gate 1 check | Ask: *"Does this feel like the real wound, or the surface behavior above it?"* Wait for user confirmation |

| Post-Gate Step | Action | Output / Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Format selection | Confirm platform guide in `story-formats/` (`longform/personal-essay.md`, `linkedin/`, etc.) | Format confirmed |
| Story Brief | Present opening hook, core scene, emotional arc, and silent ending | **Gate 2:** Wait for user approval before drafting |

```text
Recover Facts -> Desire & Contradiction -> Integrity + Research -> Gate 1 (Conflict) -> Format -> Story Brief -> Gate 2 (Brief) -> Draft
```
