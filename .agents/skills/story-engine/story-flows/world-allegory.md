# Flow: World & Allegory

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Use when | Parable, allegory, fiction, or philosophical topic governed by a consistent symbolic law |
| Do not use | Straight personal memoir (`personal-reflective.md`) or direct opinion post (`thesis-perspective.md`) |
| World source | [`../world/README.md`](../world/README.md) and [`../world/worldmaking-framework.md`](../world/worldmaking-framework.md) |
| Interview cadence | Ask 2–4 focused questions per round in user's language; never draft during interrogation |
| Evidence discipline | Label details as observed, remembered, sourced, proposed, or fictional; never manufacture nonfiction facts |

| Round | Goal | Questions / Fields to Capture |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Trajectory | Topic & emotion | Central question; factual basis; emotion in experience vs. narrator now; reader arc (opening → center → ending); what stays unresolved |
| 2. Governing Law | Select world | Read [`../world/README.md`](../world/README.md); what is threatened or falsely promised? What does action accomplish? |
| Gate 1 | Confirm law | Present World Fit Assessment (recommended world, law, evidence, closest alternative, open contradictions); wait for confirmation |
| 3. Symbols | 8-symbol ledger | Read full `../world/<name>-world.md`; ground every primitive: manifestation, specifics, function, relationship, shadow/pressure, status |
| 4. Audit & Format | Consistency & Brief | Apply [`../world/worldmaking-framework.md`](../world/worldmaking-framework.md); pick `story-formats/`; present World Brief for **Gate 2** approval |

| World | File | Governing Law / Orientation | Use When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Happy | [`happy-world.md`](../world/happy-world.md) | Right if it increases a being's capacity to be alive | Connection, joy, rest, play, or meaning is restored or sustained |
| Suffer | [`suffer-world.md`](../world/suffer-world.md) | Right if it does not offer a way out | Enclosure holds; effort and time do not convert pain into growth |
| Forge | [`forge-world.md`](../world/forge-world.md) | Right if it asserts agency against the void | Deeds meet resistance, pay real cost, and carve form |
| Void | [`void-world.md`](../world/void-world.md) | Right if it dissolves distinction and returns to zero | Movement is toward unburdening and release rather than striving |
| Zhong | [`zhong-world.md`](../world/zhong-world.md) | Right if it meets circumstance in due measure | Attention, limits, and feedback replace rigid reaction |
| Lost | [`lost-world.md`](../world/lost-world.md) | Sudden severance of meaning and utility | Structural obsolescence hollows out a functioning system |

| Symbol Record Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Manifestation & specifics | Concrete object, action, setting, or exchange (who, where, when) |
| Function & relationship | What it enables/dissolves, and which other primitive it alters |
| Shadow & status | How its absence/inversion shows; mark `grounded`, `partial`, `missing`, or `resolved absence` |

```text
Round 1 (Trajectory) -> Round 2 (Select ../world/) -> Gate 1 -> Round 3 (8 Symbols) -> Framework Audit -> Gate 2 -> Draft
```
