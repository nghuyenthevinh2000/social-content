# Flow: Thesis & Perspective

| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Use when | Contrarian take, metaphor/paradox commentary, industry observation, analytical essay, or social POV post |
| Do not use | Personal life event (`personal-reflective.md`) or symbolic/fictional world (`world-allegory.md`) |
| Anti-cliché rule | Never stop at "people do X wrong"; uncover the incentive or fear making smart people do X |

| Stage | Focus | Questions / Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Surface vs. Fear | Hidden incentive | What obvious behavior is critiqued? What uncomfortable fear, ego, or incentive keeps smart people doing it? |
| 2. Concrete Proof | Named real cases | Verify **named real-life references** via `search_web` (companies, events, exact numbers) for each point; no generic hypotheticals |
| 3. Cost of Blindness | Real stakes | Who pays the price if nothing changes, and what exactly breaks when reality hits? |
| 4. Earned Shift | Actionable alternative | What specific shift in mindset or behavior replaces the broken pattern without promising easy magic? |
| 5. Research | Practitioner words | Search real debates/frustrations (`search_web`) for unpolished vocabulary and counter-arguments |

| Assessment Field | What to Produce Before Gate 1 |
|---|---|
| Surface critique | The visible behavior or metaphor being examined |
| Root paradox / fear | The deeper psychological or structural incentive behind it |
| Proving mechanisms | Concrete examples/patterns that hold up under scrutiny |
| Reader shift | What the reader stops defending and starts doing differently |
| Gate 1 check | Ask: *"Does this name the real tension behind the topic, or is it still a surface critique?"* Wait for confirmation |

| Format Option | Guide in `story-formats/` | Best Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Long-form Essay | [`longform/personal-essay.md`](../story-formats/longform/personal-essay.md) | Paradox hook → concrete mechanisms → earned shift → resonant close |
| LinkedIn (Alić) | [`linkedin/jasmin-alic-formats.md`](../story-formats/linkedin/jasmin-alic-formats.md) | Hook + Rehook; Contrarian, List, Insider, or Proof Point format |
| LinkedIn (Jescil-Richard) | [`linkedin/jescil-richard-formats.md`](../story-formats/linkedin/jescil-richard-formats.md) | Bang opening, active verbs, one-person focus, punch ending |
| Other | `story-formats/<platform>/` | Present Story Brief (with named real-life proof per point) for **Gate 2** approval; run `humanizer` after drafting |

```text
Paradox & Fear -> Named Real Proof + Research -> Gate 1 -> Format -> Brief (Gate 2) -> Draft -> Humanize
```
