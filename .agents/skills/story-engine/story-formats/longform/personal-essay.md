# Personal Blog Essay

Use for first-person reflective blog posts.

## Required inputs

- Platform and intended reader
- Lived experience the writer can truthfully tell
- Root conflict confirmed after Phase 1
- One concrete scene that carries the emotional turn
- Intended insight, without making a universal claim

## Story Brief

Before drafting, present and obtain approval for:

1. Working title
2. Reader and central question
3. Opening scene
4. Four-part arc: friction, lived experience, insight, practical implication
5. Ending image or line
6. Claims to qualify or avoid

## Draft conventions

- Open in a specific scene, not an abstract thesis.
- Keep the writer's experience distinct from general advice.
- Let sensory detail carry the change before naming its meaning.
- Use research only to add context; do not make medical or causal claims beyond the evidence.
- End with a question, choice, or image that leaves room for the reader.
