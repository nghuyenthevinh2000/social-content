# Story Brainstorming

- [Story Integrity Interrogation](integrity-interrogation.md) tests the story before drafting.
- [Reflective Writing](reflective-writing.md) combines research and practical guidance for turning past experience into clear insight.
- [Story World Interrogation](world-interrogation.md) runs independently: it selects a world from the topic and emotional intent, then asks for missing symbolic details until its architecture is complete and approved. No other brainstorming method is required.
- [World Definitions](world/README.md) supplies the laws, primitives, and exclusions used in that interview.
