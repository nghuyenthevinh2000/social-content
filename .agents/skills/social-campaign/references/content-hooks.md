## 1. Hook Selection Architecture

| Driver Category | Psychological Mechanism | When to Deploy | Typical Retention / Conversion Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cognitive Gap & Open Loops** | Deprivation of closure; the brain cannot ignore an incomplete narrative loop. | Top-of-Funnel (TOFU) awareness; newsletters; long-form video intros. | Maximizes 3-second view rate and click-through on `...see more`. |
| **Contrarian & Belief-Flip** | Cognitive dissonance; challenges conventional industry wisdom or standard advice. | Thought leadership; establishing differentiated brand positioning. | Drives polarizing comment sections, viral reposts, and high debate velocity. |
| **Loss Aversion & Negative Framing** | Pain avoidance; humans are 2x more motivated to prevent losses than acquire gains. | Problem agitation; compliance warnings; security, tax, and legal advisory. | Generates immediate scroll-stop and elevated bookmark/save rates. |
| **Data, Numbers & Extreme Specificity** | Cognitive anchoring; exact numbers imply empirical truth and scientific rigor. | Case studies; SaaS product demos; ROI breakdowns; financial analyses. | Builds immediate credibility; stops technical and executive buyers. |
| **Identity Callout & Exclusion** | Self-categorization theory; forcing micro-segments to raise their hands. | Account-based marketing; niche courses; role-specific playbooks. | Filters unqualified viewers; produces 3x higher conversion among target persona. |
| **Inside Access & Industry Secrets** | Exclusivity bias; human desire for backstage access to gatekept knowledge. | Teardowns; competitor benchmarking; tool stacks; growth playbooks. | High virality, community shares, and external link clicks. |
| **Narrative "Jenga" / In Media Res** | Cinematic suspense; dropping the audience directly into a high-stakes turning point. | Personal branding; founder origin stories; transformation case studies. | High average watch time and completion rate across long-form video. |
| **Kinetic Pattern Interrupt** | Visual orienting response; breaking feed scrolling autopilot in 0.5–1.5 seconds. | TikTok; Instagram Reels; YouTube Shorts; feed carousels. | Prevents initial thumb swipe; creates critical 2-second retention window. |

## 3. Visual & Kinetic Pattern Interrupts

| Interrupt Type | First 0.5–1.5s Execution | Psychological Impact | Best Media Channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Mid-Sentence Jump Cut** | Starts speaking mid-word with audio already active; camera cuts abruptly from 1.0x to 1.3x zoom. | Eliminates feed preamble; tricks audience into believing they arrived in the middle of crucial information. | TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| **The Prop Reframe** | Holding an unrelated physical object (lemon, contract paper, rubber duck, brick) directly up to camera lens before pulling back. | Breaks visual scrolling monotony; provokes subconscious "Why is that there?" curiosity. | Short-form vertical video (9:16) |
| **The Incomplete Visual / Crisis Screen** | Screen recording showing a sudden red error message, crashing chart line, or shredded document before showing the creator. | Triggers immediate loss aversion and curiosity before spoken words register. | Product demos, financial reviews, legal commentary |
| **The Rapid Negative Reaction** | Creator shaking head in disbelief, aggressively crossing out text on a whiteboard, or ripping a printed sheet. | Conveys visceral emotional resonance and contrarian rejection of common practices. | Educational reels, teardown carousels |
| **High-Contrast Monochrome Banner** | First slide of a carousel featuring heavy dark ink typography over stark cream/alabaster canvas with zero clutter. | Eliminates feed color noise; signals dense, high-signal reading material. | LinkedIn carousels, Instagram swipe decks |
| **The "Sticky Note" / Screenshot Overlay** | Placing a raw iOS Notes screenshot, Tweet card, or Slack notification right over video footage. | Leverages familiar UI credibility; audiences instinctively read native UI elements. | TikTok, Reels, Twitter media cards |

## 4. Platform Execution & Technical Constraints

| Platform | Primary Hook Zone | Maximum Hook Window | Critical Technical Guardrails |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Facebook Page** | Lines 1–2 above the fold (pre-`See More`) & 9:16 / 4:5 image title cards | First 80–120 characters | Avoid link in body text if reach is prioritized; hook must stand alone without clicking preview. |
| **LinkedIn** | First 3 lines before the `...see more` fold (approx. 140–210 characters) | First 3 lines (2–3 seconds scanning) | Use line breaks after line 1; avoid heavy emoji spam; high-contrast PDF carousels perform 2–3x over static links. |
| **TikTok / Reels** | First 1.5–3 seconds of spoken audio + large subtitle overlay in vertical safe zone | 1.5–3.0 seconds maximum | Keep titles inside 9:16 safe margins (avoid bottom 20% comment zone and right 15% action icons); fast audio start. |
| **Twitter / X** | Opening tweet of thread or first 100 characters of single post | First 2 lines of tweet | Standalone hook tweet must provide standalone insight; avoid external links in tweet #1 (place in reply). |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Dynamic motion in first 1.0s + large high-contrast visual title card | First 2.0 seconds | High audio volume normalization; loop ending back to hook opening sentence for replay boost. |
