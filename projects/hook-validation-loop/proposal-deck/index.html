Controlling Noise in Organic Reach

Organic social distribution is noisy. Without scientific controls, AI loops learn from pure luck. We enforce five rules.

RULE 01

Isolate the Opening

Only the first 3 seconds change. The video body, audio, caption, and hashtags remain identical so we know what moved the needle.

RULE 02

Match Time Slots

Variants go live in equivalent daily time slots to avoid comparing a morning post with an off-peak midnight upload.

RULE 03

Normalize to Baseline

Raw view counts are misleading. Hook Score measures 3-second retention relative to the account's rolling 30-day median.

RULE 04

Honest Confidence

Every test is labeled Clear Winner, Leaning, or Inconclusive. If results are inconclusive, we rotate the video instead of guessing.

RULE 05

Require Replication

A hook angle must win across at least two separate rounds before it graduates into the brand's validated playbook.
