# AI Builder Cup 2026: Contest Requirements & Challenge Themes

> **Source:** [AI Builder Cup - Challenges & Requirements](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html)  
> **Program:** Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026 (powered by Hack2skill / Gen AI Academy APAC Edition)  
> **Motto:** *"Build in JAPAC. Build for the world."*

---

## 1. Challenge Themes

Every team must build against **one** of the six challenge themes. Choose the one closest to the problem statement you are solving:

### 4. Media, Content & Digital Experiences *(Primary Target for Hook Validation Loop)*

- **Goal:** Build an AI-powered solution transforming how content is created, discovered, personalized, understood, or experienced.
- **Focus Areas:** Media, entertainment, publishing, education, communication, social media, or other digital content ecosystems.
- **Application Note:** Perfectly fits the **Hook Validation Loop** (AI-driven generation, real-world distribution, metrics diagnosis, and automated feedback loop for organic content).

---

## 2. Mandatory Technical & Tooling Requirements

Submissions must deliver a **functional, working prototype** demonstrating a clear idea in action—pitch decks and static mockups alone will not qualify.

1. **Google AI Models:**
   - Must leverage Google AI models, specifically the **Gemini** family (e.g., Gemini 1.5 Pro / Flash) or **Gemma** open models.
2. **Agentic Platforms:**
   - Must be built using agentic platforms/frameworks such as **Google Cloud Agent Platform**, **Antigravity**, or **Google AI Studio**.
3. **Deployment on Google Cloud:**
   - The functional prototype must be hosted and running on Google Cloud Platform via **Cloud Run** or **Firebase**.

---

## 3. Mandatory Submission Deliverables

When submitting the solution, the following items are required:

| Deliverable | Format / Specs | Key Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Clear Proposal** | Pitch Deck / PPT exported as **PDF** | • State the compelling vision and positive impact.<br>• Clearly articulate problem statement and community/user value.<br>• Demonstrate technical feasibility and scaling potential. |
| **2. Functional Prototype** | Working Live Application URL | • Built with Google Cloud AI tools & Gemini/Gemma models.<br>• Deployed on Cloud Run or Firebase.<br>• Effectively showcases core end-to-end functionality. |
| **3. Video Demonstration** | **3-Minute** Public Video (YouTube, Vimeo, or Google Drive) | • Walkthrough and demo showing the AI creation in action.<br>• Must demonstrate real features, architecture, and user workflow. |
| **4. Category Specification** | Metadata / Form Selection | • Explicitly tag the selected theme / category (e.g., *Media, Content & Digital Experiences*). |

---

## 4. General Submission Standards

- **Functionality:** Prototype must be operational and demonstrate an end-to-end working loop addressing the selected challenge.
- **Platform Compatibility:** Clean integration with Google Cloud architecture, ensuring performance, resilience, and horizontal scalability.
- **Language:** All submission materials (code, presentations, documentation, and video narration) must be in **English**.

---

## 5. Evaluation & Scoring Criteria

Submissions are evaluated against weighted evaluation criteria:

1. **Meaningful Use of Gen AI:**
   - How effectively Generative AI is incorporated into the core loop. Does Gen AI directly drive the solution rather than serving as a superficial add-on?
2. **Technical Implementation:**
   - Soundness of system architecture, code execution, reliability, and deployment on GCP (Cloud Run / Firebase).
3. **Scalability & Sustainability:**
   - Potential for wider real-world adoption, unit economics, and long-term viability beyond hackathon stage.
4. **Problem Alignment:**
   - Relevance and depth in addressing the specific industry theme.
5. **Potential Impact:**
   - Clear, measurable value delivered to creators, businesses, or communities.
6. **Originality & Differentiation:**
   - Novel approach and clear competitive moat separating the solution from existing market players.
7. **Innovation in Gen AI:**
   - Creative application of multi-agent orchestration, reasoning, or closed-loop feedback systems.
8. **Usability & Accessibility:**
   - Intuitive, frictionless UX for non-technical or creator workflows.
9. **Experience & Cohesion:**
   - End-to-end seamless integration between data ingestion, AI diagnostics, and asset generation.
