## 1. Creative directions to investigate

| Direction | Viewer experience | Required resource / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Material intimacy | Close-up paper movement, surface detail and handling sounds; curiosity becomes sensory closeness. | Real sheets filmed under changing light. Do not fabricate texture or moving reflections from a photograph. |
| Workshop trial | Observe a printing or folding trial; uncertainty becomes informed control. | Equipment, operator access and documented conditions. Show the actual outcome; never assume success or universal compatibility. |
| Sheet to application | Discover the invitation, envelope, bag or box behind the sheet; possibility becomes professional recognition. | Genuine finished pieces with confirmed stock provenance. No invented sheet-to-product transformation. |
| Human evaluation | Watch someone assess samples for an actual job; distance becomes collaborative confidence. | A willing participant and real evaluation task. No staged testimonial presented as spontaneous opinion. |

| Previous assumption | Rebuild rule |
|---|---|
| Female worker and traditional papermaking | Optional only if appropriate, authentic and verified; neither is required for the new film. |
| Male viewers aged 25–35 | Not a validated buying segment. Prioritize the actual stock buyer and application rather than assuming demographics establish demand. |
| Existing creator reference | Historical context, not a locked template. Select a suitable evidenced hook source before drafting a new opening. |

## 2. Production workflow

| Stage | Output / action | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Research — completed | Product/context review, 10 free web searches, seven substantive pages read, four research territories. | Research is not audiovisual verification: no reference films watched or tracks auditioned. |
| Resource discovery — next | Confirm actual buyer hesitation, genuine product evidence, filming possibilities and trial terms. | Ask the user for resources, facts or permissions the agent cannot obtain. |
| Creative proposal | Direction options, reference/mood review, emotional arc, sound approach, production method and itemized external costs. | User selects or combines directions and approves the brief and production approach. |
| Script | Timed Vietnamese words and visual beats grounded in the approved brief. | Confirm story tension and satisfy the repository's story-engine and hook-source gates before drafting; user approves script. |
| Storyboard | Shot IDs, framing, actions, durations, source assets, transitions and continuity requirements. | User approves shot plan. |
| Assets | Inspect authentic footage/specimens; prepare approved graphics and audio; generate support only if authorized. | Scene-by-scene asset review before full composition rendering. |
| Edit and sound | Assemble the picture, restrained labels, subtitles where needed, handling sounds, speech and licensed music if selected. | Preserve approved medium and factual claims; no silent fallback to a slideshow. |
| Render and QA | Check encoded dimensions, duration, frame rate, audio, readability and representative frames. | Technical success alone does not establish creative quality; record remaining defects and revise. |
| Delivery | Final MP4, editable sources, poster frame where appropriate, asset provenance and QA notes. | User reviews deliverables. Publishing requires separate explicit authorization. |

## 4. Questions for the user

| Question | Useful answer |
|---|---|
| What do workshop customers actually hesitate about? | One real question or objection about trying the paper, with an anonymized message or your account of a conversation. If unknown, keep it unknown. |
| What can you supply or record? | Actual sheets, verified finished pieces, printing/folding trials, workshop access or sample handling. Provide existing file paths, describe what can be filmed, or say filming is unavailable. |
| What does “thử giấy” offer? | Sample availability, charges/shipping and the next step after contact. Do not assume samples are free. |
| If paid support becomes necessary, what is permitted? | Approved service access and maximum spend, requested only when a concrete production route requires it. Default remains no paid calls. |
