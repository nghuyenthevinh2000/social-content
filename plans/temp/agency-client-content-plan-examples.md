## 1. Plan Architecture Overview

| Deliverable Section | Client Value | Agency Standard Content | Update Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategy & Persona Alignment** | Grounds content in business goals and customer pains | Monthly target KPI, ICP persona pains, brand tone guardrails, and competitor benchmark notes. | Quarterly or monthly refresh |
| **Pillar Weight Matrix** | Balances authority, trust, and conversion without fatigue | Explicit percentage distribution (e.g., 35% Thought Leadership, 30% Education/How-To, 20% Proof, 15% Direct Offer). | Monthly adjustment based on analytics |
| **Editorial Staging Grid** | Serves as the single source of truth for execution | Scheduled date/time, channel, pillar, full copy, hook, hashtags, visual asset links, and review status. | Bi-weekly or monthly batch |
| **Creative Brief & Asset Bank** | Ensures design team and client align on visual assets | Aspect ratio specifications (9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, 4:5 portrait, PDF carousels), raw footage requirements, and Canva/Figma links. | Accompanying each content batch |
| **Governance & Approval Protocol** | Eliminates publishing errors and legal/brand safety risks | Traffic-light status tracking, 48-hour client review SLA, change request format, and sign-off freeze. | Standing operational agreement |

## 5. Master Spreadsheet Schema

| Field Name | Type / Format | Description & Purpose | Permissions & Visibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Item_ID` | String (`B2B-2026-W01-P01`) | Unique persistent key for tracking revisions and performance | Read-only for Client |
| `Publish_Date_Time` | ISO 8601 Timestamp | Scheduled distribution time adjusted for client target timezone | Agency Editable / Client View |
| `Channel` | Multi-select enum | Target platform (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) | Agency Editable / Client View |
| `Pillar_Category` | Select enum | Thematic category (Thought Leadership, Education, Proof, Offer) | Agency Editable / Client View |
| `Funnel_Stage` | Select enum | Marketing objective: TOFU (Aware), MOFU (Consider), BOFU (Convert) | Agency Internal |
| `Primary_Hook` | Text | First 1–2 lines designed to stop scroll in feeds | Agency Editable / Client Review |
| `Full_Copy` | Long Text / Markdown | Complete caption text, line breaks, formatting, and emojis | Agency Editable / Client Inline Commenting |
| `Call_To_Action` | Text | Concrete next action required from the audience | Agency Editable / Client Review |
| `Creative_Asset_URL` | Hyperlink | Direct link to reviewable graphic, video, or carousel proof | Agency Upload / Client Review |
| `Approval_Status` | Status Enum | `Draft` ➔ `Internal QA` ➔ `Client Review` ➔ `Approved` ➔ `Scheduled` | Client Sign-off Trigger |
| `Client_Feedback` | Rich Text | Asynchronous client change requests and approval notes | Client Editable |
