1. Face: Can I run it without having to show my face if I wanted to?

Some AI income methods require you to put yourself out there and show your face, while others don’t. A good one ideally gives you the option to decide, rather than making it compulsory.

1. Advantage (I am very weak in borrowing): Can I start earning without spending years building my own audience?

The AI income methods worth paying attention to aren't the ones that require you to have an advantage before starting. They're the ones that hand you one, regardless of your background or skills. But having an advantage doesn't always mean having a specific skill.

1. Capital: Can I try it without risking much money upfront?

Some AI income methods require you to spend lots of money before you ever see a cent back, which naturally makes them a lot riskier than others. Dropshipping is a common example of this. Most people spend a few thousand dollars testing which products might sell, long before they know if any of them will. Amazon FBA works similarly. You need to invest in inventory, storage, and ad spend upfront, before a single sale happens.

1. Expertise: Can I start it now, without having to learn any skills first?

Different AI income methods require different levels of expertise, and this is not always obvious at first. Freelancing is a good example of this. Depending on the clients you work with, you might need a fairly developed skill set, or you might get by with very little experience.

1. Time: Can I succeed with it without having to spend several hours a day on it?

If you’re considering starting an AI income online on the side, you likely don’t have all the time in the world. The best income methods allow you to build them on the side. They don't ask you to quit your job or go all-in before you even know if it's going to work.

1. Scale: Can I increase my income without increasing the number of hours I work on it?

Most AI income methods have a time constraint, meaning your income potential is tied to how many hours you put in.
