# Content comparison

| Order | Compare | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Publication/creative IDs, actual hook/media, duration, audience, offer and CTA | Distinguish planned content, captions and inspected footage. |
| 2 | Reporting scope, observation time, actual age, spend and placement conditions | Unequal ages or sources make the result descriptive, not a matched experiment. |
| 3 | Views, viewers and separate Traffic/Source breakdowns | Distribution differences are not automatically creative effects. |
| 4 | Opening drop-off, average watch time, three-second views and available retention | Average percentage watched is not completion rate; incompatible denominators must not become invented rates. |
| 5 | Age/gender and verified buyer roles | Tiny demographic samples cannot establish purchasing intent. |
| 6 | Project-defined qualified inquiries, next steps and attributed outcomes | Unknown is not zero; public engagement is not conversion. |
| 7 | Observation, limitation, explanation hypothesis and one next test | Cite exact evidence and preserve historical results. No causal winner from unmatched posts. |

```text
Comparable evidence → Distribution → Attention → Qualified action → Proposed next test
```

| Review output | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence | Source file, publication/placement ID, timestamp, scope and checkpoint. |
| Positive signal | What actually improved, with its uncertainty; separate editorial strengths from measured outcomes. |
| Gap | Missing identity, timing, distribution, retention or buyer-action evidence. |
| Next test | One change, expected outcome, controls and common observation window. |
| Decision | Repeat, revise or inconclusive; human approval before publishing or changing allocation. |

| Storage | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Project comparisons | Store dated worked comparisons under `<project>/loop/`; keep metrics under `<project>/sources/post-metrics/`. |
| History | Do not overwrite tried hypotheses or treat a planned experiment as published. Follow the project's own offers, audience and factual constraints. |
