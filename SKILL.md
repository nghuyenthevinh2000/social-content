10 powerful storytelling tips I use as a storyteller? Let’s have them

🎯 Straight to the point (in no particular order):


1. K.I.S.S = Keep it simple, storyteller.
2. Write in a conducive environment.
3. Active voice = stronger impact.
4. Choose emotions over facts.
5. ONLY write to 1 person.
6. End with a punch.
7. Use a bang.
8. No distractions.
9. Have a story goal.
10. Show and don't tell us. 

How to Apply Them:

1. Start with a bang: Your first sentence should make readers curious, shocked, or intrigued. Give them no choice but to read on.


2. Show, don't tell us: Paint pictures with your words. Instead of “I was surprised,” say, “My jaw dropped when I opened the Temu product.”


3. Emotions over facts: People don’t remember statistics, they remember how something made them feel. Make them laugh, cry, belong, or reflect.


4. ONLY write to 1 person: Even if 1 million people read your story, it should feel like a private conversation with only one. 🙋🏽‍♀️


5. No distractions. A distracted storyteller creates a distracted reader. Go off IG reels (don't be shy, I see you). Focus. Get in the zone. 


6. Have a story goal: Before you write, ask yourself: What do I want my reader to feel when they finish this? Clarity changes everything.


7. Active voice = stronger impact. “She broke the glass” is more powerful than “The glass was broken by her.” Keep it sharp.


8. K.I.S.S = Keep it simple, storyteller. Complexity kills connection. Simple words, deep meaning. That’s the magic. 


9. Write in a conducive environment: The harsh truth? Your surroundings shape your writing. Find a space that lets your creativity breathe. It doesn't have to be the Effiel tower, you can choose to sit in a quiet space away from distractions. 


10. End with a punch: Your last line should linger. Make it memorable. Make it matter. 👊🏽


An Extra Tip?

Take action. Write. Thinking about storytelling isn’t storytelling, writing is.
